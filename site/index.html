FAQ

Does it use the YouTube API?

No. It works with the visible Watch Later page in your browser.

Does it permanently reorder my playlist?

No. Watched-first sorting is a visual sort for loaded rows only.

Does my data leave the browser?

No. There are no analytics, ads, external servers, cookies, tokens, or account credentials.
